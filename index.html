About Me

Hi, I'm Yike Jin. I am an undergraduate student at Zhejiang University, majoring in Industrial Design in the College of Computer Science & Technology.

My research interests lie in GUI agents, multimodal large language models, and lifelong agent learning. I am particularly interested in how agents acquire reusable skills, organize their memory, and improve through interaction.

Research Overview

How can agents turn experience into reusable skills and lasting knowledge?

01 / SKILLS

Self-evolving skill systems

Supporting the full skill lifecycle for autonomous GUI agents: discovery, acquisition, retrieval, execution, evaluation, refinement, reuse, and composition across applications.

02 / MEMORY

Parametric agent memory

Compressing skills, procedural knowledge, and interaction trajectories into model parameters or lightweight adapters for efficient retrieval, continual updates, and scalable long-term memory.

Current researchIn progress · Jul 2026–

Self-Evolving Skill Learning for Web Agents

Yike Jin*, Hongming Piao*, Ying Wei

Exploring a closed loop of capability-driven exploration, skill acquisition, composition, and validation. The framework adaptively selects textual, parametric, or hybrid skill representations and is being evaluated on WebArena.

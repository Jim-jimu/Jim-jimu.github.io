Research

I focus on two connected directions:

  • Self-evolving skill systems: the full skill lifecycle for autonomous GUI agents, from discovery and acquisition to evaluation, refinement, reuse, and composition across applications.
  • Parametric agent memory: storing skills, procedural knowledge, and interaction trajectories in model parameters or lightweight adapters for efficient retrieval and continual updates.
